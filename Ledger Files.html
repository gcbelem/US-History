Summer, 1929

The stock market has never been this high. A client walks into your office with $100 and one idea: buy shares on margin, which means a small down payment and borrowed money for the rest.

Four brokers have each left a prospectus on your desk. You are the investment advisor. Decide where the client's money should go.

Why every company was broke

The boom hid weak spots. Cars and trucks took business from the railroads. Housing starts were falling. Customers were buried in installment debt. Farm prices kept dropping. Share prices rose because people borrowed to buy them, not because the companies earned more.

Discuss in your pair

  1. Which red flag should have stopped you, and which one did you ignore?
  2. A rival's rumor can be a lie, but all four rumors were true. How could you have checked?
  3. If every company is weak, what should a careful investor have done in 1929?
